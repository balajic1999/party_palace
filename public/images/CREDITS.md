## Two things to settle before launch

**1. These are not photographs of Party Palace.**
They are other venues and generic celebration shots. That is fine for a demo,
but a customer who books after seeing a 200-seat auditorium and turns up to a
private room has been misled — and it invites bad reviews. Replace these with
photographs of the actual rooms before the site goes public. Swapping is a
one-line change per entry; see `README.md` in this folder.

**2. Some images contain identifiable people.**
`pkg-couple.jpg`, `g-audience.jpg` and `g-celebration.jpg` show recognisable
faces. The Pexels License covers the photograph, but it does **not** grant a
model release — using a stranger's face to advertise a business can raise
personality-rights problems. Either replace those three with real customers who
have given written permission, or use shots without identifiable faces.

The remaining images (rooms, decor, candles, food) have no such issue.
